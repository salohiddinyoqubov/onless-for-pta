# Verified product UI evidence

These six images show real checked-in Onless product interfaces rendered with deterministic synthetic or bundled offline data. They are evidence of the complete private product UI; they are not screenshots of the smaller runnable slices in this public repository.

No image was generated, composited, retouched, cropped to hide UI, or altered through screenshot-time DOM/CSS injection. Supported local Next.js and Expo runtime preferences disabled their development indicators before the accepted captures; product components, styles, DOM/native view tree, and pixels were not edited to hide them. Final files were encoded with `cwebp -q 88 -metadata none`. `webpinfo` reports one valid VP8 frame per file and no ICC, EXIF, XMP, GPS, animation, or decode error.

## Privacy and review boundary

The accepted frames contain no real name, handle, email, phone, school, customer record, payment record, account/session identifier, token, cookie, QR code, URL, filesystem path, IP/MAC address, or production metric. `Demo` and `Exam PC 01`–`04` are explicit synthetic identities; `DE` is the checked-in navbar's natural initials rendering for `Demo`.

Captures were produced and reviewed on 2026-08-20 (Asia/Tashkent). The independent visual gate required at least 4.5/5.0 per image and rejected a perfect 5.0 score as non-discriminating; the privacy/trust gate required 1.0/1.0. All six final hashes above passed both gates. Raw PNGs, browser state, capture scripts, APKs, traces, and temporary build workspaces are not part of this repository.
